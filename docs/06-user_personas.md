# Who We Serve

Our initial market focuses on small and medium-sized businesses across Africa that manage physical inventory.

Examples include:

- Retail stores
- Wholesale businesses
- Manufacturing companies
- Warehouses
- Pharmacies
- Distribution companies

Primary users include:

- Business owners
- Store managers
- Storekeepers
- Sales personnel
- Procurement officers
- Auditors
