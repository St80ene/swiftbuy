# SwiftBuy Design Principles

## Simplicity over Feature Quantity

Every feature must reduce operational friction.

---

## Inventory is the Source of Truth

Stock is tracked through inventory movements rather than manual quantity updates.

---

## Audit Everything

Every important action must be attributable to a user.

---

## Workflow over CRUD

Business processes take precedence over simple create, read, update, and delete operations.

---

## Modular Architecture

Each domain should evolve independently with minimal coupling.

---

## Build for Growth

Every MVP decision should leave room for future capabilities such as multi-warehouse support, offline synchronization, and AI-assisted procurement.
