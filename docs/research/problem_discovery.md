# Problem Discovery

## Background

The idea for SwiftBuy originated from observing how small businesses manage inventory using spreadsheets.

The first business studied was a nylon distribution business.

---

## Existing Workflow

The business currently manages inventory using Microsoft Excel.

The spreadsheet contains:

- Product Name
- Quantity Sold
- Quantity in Stock

The workbook is shared between multiple people.

---

## Problems Observed

### Inconsistent Stock Levels

Multiple people update the spreadsheet independently.

This leads to:

- incorrect inventory counts
- conflicting edits
- duplicate entries

---

### Lack of Accountability

There is no way to determine:

- who changed inventory
- when it was changed
- why it changed

---

### Manual Procurement

Purchase decisions rely on memory rather than inventory metrics.

This causes:

- delayed purchases
- overstocking
- stock shortages

---

### No Real-time Visibility

Managers cannot immediately determine:

- available stock
- low stock items
- inventory value
- pending purchases

---

## Opportunity

Digitize inventory management while maintaining simplicity for businesses transitioning from spreadsheets.
