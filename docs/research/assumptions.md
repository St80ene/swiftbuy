# Product Assumptions

## Assumption 1

Businesses already know their products.

SwiftBuy does not attempt product discovery.

---

## Assumption 2

Inventory accuracy is more important than feature quantity.

---

## Assumption 3

Businesses require accountability.

Every critical action should be auditable.

---

## Assumption 4

Inventory changes should always have a reason.

No stock should change silently.

---

## Assumption 5

Procurement should follow an approval workflow.

Unauthorized purchases increase operational risk.
