# User Personas

## Owner

Needs:

- Business overview
- Reports
- Profitability
- Inventory value

---

## Inventory Officer

Needs:

- Manage products
- Track stock
- Create purchase requests

---

## Storekeeper

Needs:

- Receive inventory
- Adjust stock
- Count inventory

---

## Manager

Needs:

- Approve purchases
- Monitor inventory

---

## Supplier

Needs:

- Receive purchase orders
- Confirm deliveries
