# Market Research

## Target Market

SwiftBuy targets businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets but are not ready for enterprise ERP systems.

Examples include:

- Grocery stores
- Fashion retailers
- Wholesalers
- Pharmacies
- Hardware stores

---

## Business Characteristics

Typical customers:

- 5–100 employees
- One or more suppliers
- Hundreds to thousands of products
- Inventory-driven operations

---

## Existing Alternatives

Current solutions include:

- Microsoft Excel
- Google Sheets
- Odoo
- Zoho Inventory
- ERPNext

---

## Market Gap

Many existing ERP solutions are:

- expensive
- complex
- difficult to customize
- overloaded with unnecessary features

SwiftBuy focuses on simplicity while remaining scalable.
