# MVP Decisions

Included

- Authentication
- RBAC
- Products
- Inventory
- Suppliers
- Purchase Orders
- Reports
- Dashboard
- Audit Logs

Excluded

- Payments
- POS
- Customers
- Multi-Warehouse
- Barcode Scanning
- AI Forecasting
- Accounting
- Offline Sync

Reason

Focus on solving inventory management before expanding into ERP.
