# SwiftBuy MVP Success Metrics

Success should be measured by **business outcomes**, not feature completion.

## 1. Inventory Accuracy

**Goal:** SwiftBuy reflects physical inventory reliably.

- Stock variance between system and physical count
- Number of unexplained stock discrepancies
- Frequency of stock adjustments

**Success:** Stock discrepancies become measurable, traceable, and reduce over time.

---

## 2. Inventory Visibility

**Goal:** Users can quickly know what stock they have.

- Average time to check product stock
- % of active products with current stock recorded
- Reduction in reliance on spreadsheets for stock checks

**Success:** Users can determine current stock from SwiftBuy without consulting the old system.

---

## 3. Inventory Traceability

**Goal:** Every important stock change can be explained.

- % of inventory changes with a recorded reason
- % of changes linked to an actor
- Average time to investigate a discrepancy

**Success:** Users can answer:

> What changed, by how much, when, and why?

---

## 4. Procurement Efficiency

**Goal:** Improve purchasing decisions using inventory information.

- Average time from purchase request to decision
- % of purchase decisions supported by inventory history
- Number of delayed purchases caused by missing inventory information

**Success:** Managers can make purchasing decisions without manually reconstructing inventory information.

---

## 5. Stock Availability

**Goal:** Reduce avoidable stockouts.

- Number of stockout incidents
- Stockout frequency per product
- Number of repeatedly stocked-out products

**Success:** Stockouts reduce over time as inventory visibility and purchasing decisions improve.

---

## 6. Adoption

**Goal:** SwiftBuy becomes the primary inventory system.

- % of inventory operations performed in SwiftBuy
- % of purchase requests created in SwiftBuy
- % of received stock recorded in SwiftBuy
- Frequency of users reverting to spreadsheets

**Success:** Core inventory operations no longer depend on spreadsheets.

---

## 7. Accountability

**Goal:** Important business actions are attributable.

- % of critical actions with identifiable users
- % of stock adjustments with reasons
- % of purchase decisions with identifiable approvers

**Success:** Management can determine **who did what, when, and why**.

---

## North Star Metric

> **% of core inventory and purchasing decisions made using trusted SwiftBuy data.**

### MVP Success

SwiftBuy is successful when the business can:

**Know its stock → Understand its history → Identify needs → Make purchasing decisions → Receive stock → Update inventory**

without relying on its previous spreadsheet or paper-based process.
